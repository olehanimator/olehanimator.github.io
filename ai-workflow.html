AI workflow

How I use AI in my work

AI helps me develop concepts faster, create assets and automate repetitive stages of production. Direction, composition, timing and final quality stay under my control.

01

Idea and script

In brainstorming sessions with ChatGPT, I develop the concept, story and characters, and write the screenplay and the shooting script with a storyboard. For apps, I work out the product concept and UX the same way. Technical decisions and architecture I discuss with Claude.

02

Video and animation

I generate visual concepts, characters and environments and assemble them into a storyboard. When the camera work has to be precise, Claude builds a rough 3D scene with basic animation. A simple scene is built right in the browser with Three.js. A complex one is assembled in a 3D package via MCP, where I refine the camera path, timing and animation myself. The rough render then serves as the base for video-to-video generation. When an exact camera path isn’t needed, I generate the video directly from the storyboard.

03

Images, textures and 3D

For series in a consistent style, I build pipelines in ComfyUI (FLUX) and train LoRAs when needed. I generate textures for materials and models of simple props from several views, and refine the geometry by hand. Through MCP, I connect Claude to 3D packages to edit scenes and set up basic animation.

04

Virtual showrooms

I have used two approaches: generating views based on a rough 3D model, and a pipeline of panorama → camera fly-throughs → Gaussian Splatting → 3D model.

05

Editing and automation

For the YouTube channel The Bamboo Storyteller, I automated episode assembly: a script puts the illustrations and voice-over together into an After Effects composition, leaving me only the review and the render. In other projects, scripts produce a rough cut, and I refine the rhythm and transitions myself.

06

Development

I develop games and apps in Unity, as well as websites, web apps and interactive presentations. Claude helps me write code, build prototypes and edit scenes in Unity. Architecture, testing and final decisions are my responsibility.
